HOW I COACH

Coaching should teach you, not just tire you out.

I want you to understand why you are doing something, what we are trying to improve and how your body adapts to training.

We assess first, make better decisions and then build training around you — your history, your body, your goals and what you need to be able to do.

01

Strength & Capability

For people who want to move well, become stronger and stay physically capable for the long term.

  • Functional strength
  • Movement quality and control
  • Returning to training after injury
  • Strength, mobility and stability assessment
  • Building confidence in your body
  • Long-term health and physical independence

The goal is not simply to complete workouts. It is to build a body that continues to work for you.

02

Combat Performance

For martial artists and fighters who need training to support the demands of their sport.

  • Strength and power development
  • Sport-specific conditioning
  • Movement and performance assessment
  • Hamstring, hip and lower-body capacity
  • Rehabilitation-aware programming
  • Training, recovery and competition preparation

Understand what you are training, why you are training it and how it transfers to performance.
