HOW I COACH

Coaching should teach you, not just tire you out.

I want you to understand why you are doing something, what we are trying to improve and how your body adapts to training.

We assess first, make better decisions and then build training around you — your history, your body, your goals and what you need to be able to do.

ABOUT NOMADCOACH

I became a coach because I wanted answers.

I started learning about training because I wanted to understand my own body — why injuries happened, how to recover properly and how to train in a way that would keep me strong for the long term.

That curiosity eventually took me into competitive sport, personal training, nutrition, swimming and international taekwondo coaching.

Becoming a high-level athlete never changed the reason I coach. I want people to understand their bodies and make better training decisions — whether that means getting confidently off the floor later in life or preparing to perform in competition.

Good coaching is not about making every session harder. It is about understanding what needs to adapt, choosing the right training stimulus and giving the body enough time to respond.
